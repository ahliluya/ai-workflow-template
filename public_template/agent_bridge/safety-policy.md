# Safety Policy / 安全策略

- No elevated privileges, deletion, or system changes by default. / 默认不使用提权、不删除文件、不修改系统。
- Keep secrets, credentials, personal data, and private history out of the repository. / 不向仓库提交秘密、凭据、个人数据和私人历史。
- Use only approved files, commands, and services. / 仅使用已批准的文件、命令和服务。
- Require human approval for destructive, external, paid, or out-of-scope actions. / 破坏性、外部、付费或超范围操作必须获得人工批准。
- Stop and report when scope, ownership, or risk is unclear. / 范围、所有权或风险不清时停止并报告。
